**Summary (one line):**
Find out whether a stock earns its return overnight or during the session - splits returns into close-to-open and open-to-close, per ticker or across the whole S&P 500.

**Tags / keywords:** sp500, stocks, overnight returns, intraday, close to open, gap, yfinance, data analysis, finance, python

**Use cases:**
- Check whether NVDA, MU or any ticker makes its return overnight or intraday
- Rank S&P 500 companies by overnight vs intraday dominance
- Compare tech, small-cap and high-beta stocks on their overnight profile
- Reproduce the well-known overnight-return anomaly on your own date range

**docs_url:** https://github.com/MarcinDeeec/Intra-vs-Overnight-Trading-SP500

**Needs access to:** internet (Yahoo Finance, Wikipedia); Python 3.11/3.12 with yfinance, pandas, numpy, scipy, lxml, pyarrow, matplotlib. No API keys.

**Disclaimer to keep in the description:** Educational analysis on gross, adjusted Yahoo Finance data; full scans use today's index members (survivorship bias). Not investment advice.
